Modern Slavery and Human Trafficking Statement

Published: September 09, 2026

Kandha Consulting Inc. opposes modern slavery, forced labour, child labour, and human trafficking in all forms. Respect for human dignity is a condition of the business relationships we choose to enter and maintain.

Our Commitment

We will not knowingly provide services to, purchase from, or partner with an organization that uses or knowingly benefits from these practices. This commitment applies to our own operations and to our relationships with clients, suppliers, subcontractors, and other business partners.

How We Respond to Concerns

When considering a relationship, we may review credible information about an organization's labour practices and ask questions where concerns arise. Anyone acting for Kandha is expected to raise a suspected violation promptly with Kandha's leadership. We will assess credible concerns, seek relevant facts, and take appropriate action. Where involvement is substantiated, we will decline the relationship or end it, subject to steps needed to protect affected people and meet applicable obligations.

Expectations for People Working on Our Behalf

We expect people working on Kandha's behalf to work voluntarily, to be free to leave their employment in accordance with applicable law, and to retain control of their identity documents. We do not tolerate coercion, threats, debt bondage, deceptive recruitment, or the exploitation of children.

Raise a Concern

Concerns about Kandha or a business relationship can be sent to dhivyakamalakannan@kandha.ca . We will handle reports sensitively and limit disclosure to those who need to assess and address the concern.

This statement describes Kandha's commitments. It is not an annual report under Canada's Fighting Against Forced Labour and Child Labour in Supply Chains Act.
