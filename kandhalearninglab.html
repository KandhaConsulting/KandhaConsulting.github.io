Kandha Learning Lab

A successful Dayforce implementation does more than launch a system. It gives people the knowledge and confidence to use it well. Kandha Learning Lab brings our practical, hands-on approach to learning into one place.

Learning built around your work

Every organization has its own processes, policies, and ways of working. We focus on the tasks your teams actually perform, using clear explanations, realistic examples, and guidance that connects Dayforce configuration to everyday decisions.

Our goal: self-sustaining teams

We believe the best training leaves your people more capable after the session ends. We help teams understand the “why” behind a process, document what they learn, and build the confidence to solve future challenges.

What can your team learn?

Dayforce foundations

Build a practical understanding of the system, your organization’s setup, and how processes connect across teams.

Role-based learning

Help administrators, managers, and end users learn the steps and decisions that matter in their roles.

Process and configuration

Explore how business requirements translate into configuration and how to maintain processes as needs change.

Guides and knowledge sharing

Turn working knowledge into useful procedures, checklists, and training materials your team can return to.

The Learning Lab reflects Kandha’s belief that knowledge should be shared, practiced, and passed on. If your team has a Dayforce learning challenge, we would love to understand it.
